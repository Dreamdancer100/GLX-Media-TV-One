## 📖 About GLX Media TV One

### Your media library right on the TV

GLX Media TV One needs no server, no PC and no cloud. The app runs on its own on your Android TV box or Google TV and reads your films, series, music and audio books straight from a USB drive, a USB stick or the NAS of your FritzBox. Your files are only read, never changed.

### Ideal for parents, holiday homes and second TVs

Plug in the drive, choose the folder, done. GLX Media TV One is just right when there is no server in the house, for example at your parents, in a holiday home, in a camper van or on a second TV.

### Covers and folder view

GLX Media TV One shows your collection just the way you sorted it on the drive. If a folder contains a picture, it is used as the cover. Films, series, music and audio books are detected per folder automatically.

### GLX Player with VLC

Films play in the GLX Player with the VLC engine, so MKV with AC3 or DTS sound works too. Audio track and subtitles are chosen right in the player.

### New player controls

The player controls are slim and show clear icons only: previous title, rewind, play or pause, fast forward and next title. Below you see position, remaining time and total running time. The title only appears when you pause. Repeat and shuffle are switched with a single button.

### D-pad like Kodi

While a film is playing, left and right rewind and fast forward, up and down jump 10 minutes forward or back, without any menu.

### Resume and markers

GLX Media TV One remembers where you stopped. In the player you set a marker at your favourite spot. On the next start the app asks whether you want to resume, start from the marker or play from the beginning.

### GLX Remote: your phone as a remote control

GLX Remote turns your phone into a remote control: d-pad, touchpad, keyboard, playback keys and jumps straight to your libraries. For Android there is the GLX Remote app, which you download right in the TV app under Settings › Remote control. On the iPhone you open the address shown in Safari, tap Share and then "Add to Home Screen". The remote layout can only be edited with a double tap, so nothing moves by accident.

### Fits every TV

Size and screen margin are set right in the app, so nothing is cut off at the edge. Plus 12 GLX designs, German and English. The app also runs on older 32-bit TVs.

### Built-in updates

The app updates itself. New versions only come from glx-tools.de and are checked with a checksum.

### Trial and activation

You can try GLX Media TV One free for 14 days with all features. After that you can still browse your collection, but playback needs a licence. Nothing is deleted. The licence is a one-time 19.99 euros, no subscription. After purchase you receive a GLX token by email, which you enter in the app under Settings › Licence.

### GLX Media TV One or GLX Media Server?

If you have a GLX Media Server at home, use the free app GLX Media Android TV. GLX Media TV One is made for TVs without a server and works completely on its own.
